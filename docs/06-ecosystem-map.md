## Gaps vs Base/Solana winners
- **DeFi depth is near-zero outside a single Morpho rail: lending, DEX liquidity, and yield products that anchor Base/Solana are barely present, leaving room for simple savings/yield apps** — DefiLlama lists ~31 protocols on World Chain with ~$30.2M chain TVL vs 932 protocols and ~$5,651M TVL on Base; top World Chain entries are Binance CEX ($84M), Morpho Blue ($28.6M), Uniswap (~$1.5M combined).
- **No x402 / HTTP-402 machine micropayments standard visible anywhere in the World ecosystem, while that is the fastest-growing app category on Base/Solana** — Full scrape of world.org/ecosystem returned 494 app listings; none is an x402 pay-per-call API marketplace or agent-payment storefront. Meanwhile x402 crossed 196M transactions / $42M settled by Nov 2026 with ~45% on Base and Visa/Mastercard/Stripe backing.
- **Native prediction markets are tiny: the category exists only via US-regulated Kalshi/Polymarket bridges; on-chain prediction TVL is ~0** — X3X (the only prediction-market protocol listed for World Chain on DefiLlama) shows ~0 TVL; native games (Mantica, Yoso, Canopy, Ground Truth) are small mini apps, not liquidity venues.
- **Token/memecoin launchpad economy is absent by design, unlike Base (Clanker) or Solana (pump.fun) — and partially blocked by policy** — Airdrop/claim apps dominate, but developer playbook lists 'NO token pre-sales' as a rejection trigger; only a ~0-TVL 'Meme Wallet' launchpad appears on DefiLlama.
- **Creator-economy / content monetization (Zora/Farcaster-style mints, paid posts, subscriptions) is underdeveloped** — Among 494 ecosystem listings only marginal apps (Scribstack, Tessera, World Billboard, Post Chain) do creator monetization; no social-token economy exists.
- **Simple group-money utilities are missing: no split-the-bill, group savings/ROSCA, escrow or subscription-management apps found** — Catalog scan found only solo savings (Nest, Cash Vault) and the single 'Coverage, one tap away' insurance app; World's own Morpho blog pitches 'reputation-based lending circles' and 'pay-as-you-go insurance' as unbuilt possibilities.
- **Long-tail quality is low: flagship mini apps are run by funded teams while community apps vary, so a polished micro-fee utility can still stand out in a 500+ app store** — 2026 guide: 'By May 2026, World App's Mini App directory carries more than 250 published applications... the long tail is community-built and varies in quality.'

Incubators: World Build cohorts (FWB) — WB2 winners (Fram3s, Valor, Whole World Catalog) got Buenos Aires retreat + virtual program; World Build 3 (2026) has a $20K 4-day hackathon, Seoul Build Week, 3-month program, SF demo day; alumni raised $15M+ from VCs (https://world.org/blog/announcements/build-once-deploy-anywhere-minikit-2.0-is-live-for-world-developers, https://news.bitcoin.com/world-unveils-new-toolkit-expands-developer-program-with-world-build-3). World One residency: 3 months, $10K/month, up to 5 solo SF builders (https://world.org/one). Builder perks: Alchemy free enterprise tier + $500–2K credits, Cloudflare for Startups, thirdweb trial, Oculus analytics (~10K users routed to early apps) (https://docs.world.org/mini-apps/more/community-tools-perks, https://docs.world.org/mini-apps/ecosystem/ecosystem). Direct monetization: mini apps can charge fees/subscriptions/ads/commissions; payments run peer-to-peer between the user's self-custody wallet and the developer's wallet in USDC or WLD only — app-native tokens are NOT allowed as a payment method; gas is sponsored by World App via paymaster (Pimlico), so users rarely see fees (https://strapi.aurum.law/uploads/World_Mini_Apps_Developer_Legal_Guidelines_2026_d55dbc64a1.pdf, https://www.pimlico.io/case-studies/world-app). MiniKit 2.0 (April 2026) makes web app → mini app conversion ~2 lines (EIP-1193 aligned). Tax: developers owe tax/VAT/GST where resident and where users are."

    "Known rejection triggers (developer-reported checklist): chance-based games / RNG-determined prizes, token pre-sales, paid memberships that boost yield, 'official' wording, World logos/branding, NFT purchase buttons, and using 'World' in the app name (https://github.com/ElliotFriedman/harvest-world/blob/main/docs/infra-checklist.md).",
