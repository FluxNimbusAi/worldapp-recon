# Base consumer-app winners — non-game, high-retention (lane 1 lane)

_Compiled 2026-11-12 by Nimbus recon swarm. 12 winner apps, 4 transferable concepts. All claims carry sources; items marked UNVERIFIED lack a primary confirmation._

## Noice — payments/tipping + content monetization (build size: 2/5)
- **What:** Farcaster/Base mini app that turns your likes, comments, recasts and follows into automatic USDC (or any held token) micro-tips from your own wallet to creators; creators can also price access to their time/attention (DMs, calls) as tip-gated unlocks.
- **Traction:** noice.so (late 2026, self-reported): $2,140,732 GMV, 12.75M transactions facilitated, 83,324 creators made money, 9,578 unique tippers, 83K wallets. Dune Digest 012: 281K tips from 2,886 unique wallets totaling $22.9K in its first 10 days, average tip $0.08, ~90% return rate. Featured as a flagship mini app in Coinbase's Base App launch (base.org).
- **Revenue:** Tips come out of users' own funds; $NOICE token for governance/incentives (CoinMarketCap: FDV ~$855K, 24h vol $6.6K — token economy is thin). Explicit per-tip take-rate not published (UNVERIFIED). Coinbase waived USDC transaction fees within chats at launch (CNBC, Jul 2025).
- **Retention loop:** Tipping fires automatically on the habit users already have (liking posts); ~90% return rate in Dune cohort; creators keep posting because engagement converts to income; tip budgets must be re-funded, creating a deposit-and-spend cycle.
- Source: https://noice.so/

## Tipn — payments/tipping (build size: 1/5)
- **What:** Like-to-tip mini app: set a per-like tip amount (e.g. 2 cents) and a USDC budget cap; every liked cast pays its author instantly on Base.
- **Traction:** Bankless (Jul 16, 2025): 'the most trending mini app period' on Farcaster at time of writing; Season 1 of 'Tipn Allocations' lets eligible users tip $TIPN directly from a treasury. No published user/volume totals (UNVERIFIED).
- **Revenue:** Treasury-funded token allocation seasons; no public take-rate (UNVERIFIED).
- **Retention loop:** Budget top-ups + seasonal airdrop allocation programs; social validation loop (tipping is public).
- Source: https://www.bankless.com/read/3-farcaster-tipping-mini-apps

## Degen (degen.tips) — payments/tipping (build size: 2/5)
- **What:** Social tipping app on Farcaster using the $DEGEN community token: load a Farplet wallet, set a tip allowance per like, tips flow to authors of casts you engage with.
- **Traction:** DEGEN is 'the de-facto tipping token inside Farcaster' (Ryder.id, 2026); backend rebuilt in Jul 2025 for Base App + Farcaster mini apps with zero fees on tips announced until the lottery launch (CoinMarketCap AI news, Jul 2025); treasury-backed tip-allowance model 'made $DEGEN a sensation' (Bankless).
- **Revenue:** Stated plan: 10% take on every tip facilitated, routed to a weekly multi-winner lottery (Bankless).
- **Retention loop:** Tips-per-like + lottery tickets earned per tip + daily claim/streak apps (DEGEN Daily); posting quality monetized, so creators return to earn and spenders return for the draw.
- Source: https://www.bankless.com/read/3-farcaster-tipping-mini-apps

## Blackbird — loyalty/rewards/stamp cards + restaurant payments (build size: 5/5)
- **What:** 'Eat-to-earn' shared loyalty network: diners earn $FLY points by paying for meals at ~independent restaurants (no fees or account linking for guests), redeem points for free dishes, welcome drinks and last-minute tables; Blackbird Pay settles checks in-app over Flynet, an L3 running on Base; FlyCard (with Rain) extends rewards to any Visa restaurant.
- **Traction:** $50M Series B (Apr 2025, Spark Capital + Coinbase Ventures, Amex Ventures, a16z) on ~1,000 restaurants, $85M total raised (TechCrunch); 0→10,000 daily transactions during 2024 and 242M+ $FLY distributed (Blackbird Flynet launch post, Feb 2025); restaurants pay $89/month and adoption grew 10x in year one (Fortune).
- **Revenue:** Flat 2% network fee on dining payments vs ~3.75% card interchange, with 1.5% of topline returned to restaurants as a loyalty rebate (Flynet announcement); $89/mo merchant SaaS; premium Blackbird Club tiers.
- **Retention loop:** Points accrue every meal and can only be redeemed at restaurants, forcing return visits; tiered Club perks (guaranteed reservations, secret menus) gamify frequency; shared points across venues beats single-chain silos.
- Source: https://techcrunch.com/2025/04/08/blackbird-gobbles-up-50m-for-its-blockchain-based-payment-loyalty-app-for-restaurants/

## Limitless — prediction markets (non-sportsbook) (build size: 5/5)
- **What:** Base-native prediction market with hourly/daily/weekly binary Yes/No markets on crypto and stock prices (down to 30-60 min), order-book trading (CLOB), automatic Pyth-oracle resolution, LMTS token for staking/governance/rewards.
- **Traction:** Independent DeFiRate monthly series: $5.7M volume (Aug 2025) → $204.9M (Jan 2026) → $538.5M (Mar 2026) → record $1.66B (Apr 2026) (PredictorHQ review). Crawlux April-2026 ranking: 71,203 unique users, $205M April taker volume, $1.51M April fees — third-largest onchain prediction platform behind Polymarket and Kalshi. $10M seed (Oct 2025, 1confirmation + Coinbase Ventures); reported CFTC DCM application spring 2026.
- **Revenue:** Taker fees per market (April run-rate ~$1.5M/mo), makers free; LMTS staking economy.
- **Retention loop:** Hourly/expiring markets create daily check-in cadence; points seasons drove growth (Onchain Atlas cautions volume is heavily incentive-driven and Base-native share of the category overall is small — ~0.5% of monthly notional per Pollak-cited Dune data in Jul).
- Source: https://predictorhq.com/reviews/limitless/

## HyperSplit — group bills/splitting (build size: 2/5)
- **What:** iOS app for splitting real checks: AI receipt scan, share a link with the group, each friend pays their exact share in-app, settles instantly in USDC on Base with Coinbase integration; deposits via Apple Pay.
- **Traction:** Live on US App Store (id 6752544903); named a leading stablecoin bill-split option on Base by Coca's 2026 comparison, which itself lists HyperSplit's user ratings as 'Early web app, N/A' — no public user or volume figures (UNVERIFIED).
- **Revenue:** App fee not publicly listed (per coca.xyz comparison); presumed on deposit/settlement margin (UNVERIFIED).
- **Retention loop:** Recurring shared-expense contexts (roommates, trips, team lunches) regenerate ledger balances that must be settled in-app.
- Source: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/hypersplit/id6752544903

## Splits (splits.org) — subscriptions/recurring payments + payouts (build size: 4/5)
- **What:** Open-source payment contracts that automate recurring money flows: Split (route future income by %), Liquid Split (stream by the second), Waterfall (priority payouts), plus 'Treasury' multi-sign smart-account products with invoicing and bank transfers.
- **Traction:** GitHub states $250M+ distributed, audited, zero-fee protocol deployed on 13+ chains, with full smart-account support on Ethereum, Base, Optimism, Arbitrum, Celo — and World Chain (already supports the target network).
- **Revenue:** Protocol itself charges zero fees; monetization sits in Treasury/Personal product workflows (pricing not public — UNVERIFIED).
- **Retention loop:** Once revenue streams are wired to split contracts, every future payment recurs through them automatically — the app is invisible infrastructure with built-in lock-in.
- Source: https://github.com/0xSplits

## Base App 'Pay' + Base Pay (Coinbase) — payments (P2P tap-to-pay + express checkout) (build size: 5/5)
- **What:** In-app P2P USDC on Base: NFC tap-to-pay turns any phone into a card reader with free, instant settlement; Base Pay is one-click USDC express checkout on the web, launched with Shopify and Coinbase Payment APIs/B2B payouts.
- **Traction:** Shopify enabled Base Pay for tens of thousands of merchants at launch with rollout to all merchants by end of 2025, plus 1% USDC cash-back for US shoppers (CNBC, Jul 16 2025; base.org). Allium (Nov 2026): Base organic stablecoin volume $3.99B across 12.8M transfers in 30 days, average USDC transfer $294 — consumer-scale tickets. CryptoSlate: ~32M monthly active users on Base network.
- **Revenue:** Free to users/merchants at the rail (no interchange; sidesteps 1.5-3% card fees); monetization is upstream via Coinbase on/off-ramp spread and float; Shopify/Coinbase fund the 1% cash back.
- **Retention loop:** Paying friends and buying coffee are weekly financial habits; address/shipping saved once in the Base Account makes repeat checkout one-tap.
- Source: https://www.base.org/blog/a-new-day-one

## Base Subscriptions (Coinbase Wallet/CDP primitive) — subscriptions/recurring payments (build size: 1/5)
- **What:** Users grant a bounded on-chain spend permission; the app's backend charges a fixed USDC amount per period (auto-resets), cancelable anytime — native recurring billing for SaaS and agents on Base, e.g. ENS Tools sells a 39 USDC/30-day plan on it.
- **Traction:** Shipped and documented by Coinbase (CDP docs + @base-org/account SDK: 'subscribe({recurringCharge, periodInDays})' is 3 lines of code); real deployment example at ens.tools (May-2026 writeup). Aggregate user/volume not published (UNVERIFIED).
- **Revenue:** 'No fees for merchants or users' at the protocol level — the app layer sets its own subscription margin.
- **Retention loop:** Recurring billing is the retention mechanism: one approval produces N automatic charges with zero re-auth friction.
- Source: https://docs.cdp.coinbase.com/coinbase-wallet/reference/base-pay/subscriptions-overview

## Bountycaster — earn / microtask bounty payments (build size: 2/5)
- **What:** Peer-to-peer bounty marketplace on Farcaster/X: tag @bountybot with a task and reward; others claim, deliver, and get paid on Base to their Farcaster address, with escrowed-contract and admin status flow.
- **Traction:** Long-running public Dune dashboard (ilemi/bountycaster) tracking bounties created/completed and $-value offered/completed; live examples range ~$6 (50 OP × 20 claims) to $1,000 per bounty with verified posting limits; official totals not published (UNVERIFIED magnitude).

## x402 (Coinbase micropayment protocol) — API/content micropayments (pay-per-request) (build size: 1/5)
- **What:** HTTP-402 based rail that lets apps, APIs and AI agents pay per request in USDC settled on Base — priced access to data, inference, code review, and digital services without accounts or subscriptions.
- **Traction:** Chainalysis (Oct 2026): 138.5M x402 transactions on Base in Q3 2026, up from ~58.8K a year earlier; 87% of payments worth 1¢–10¢; July: 75M payments but only $24M value; Base hosts ~85-91% of x402 volume. Reality check: Fireblocks says $42M lifetime settled (Nov 2026) and October activity fell to 5.1M txs / ~$274K, and >half of lifetime txs were PING-token farming — TRM bounds genuinely agentic share at 0.6-7.5%. 200K sellers claimed (Coinbase/agenteconomy data).
- **Revenue:** Facilitator takes per-settlement fee (~$0.001 after free tier reported for Coinbase CDP facilitator); sellers price their own micro-content.
- **Retention loop:** Metered usage: every API call re-charges automatically — the purest 'recurring micro-fee' pattern on Base, though demand is still largely speculative.
- Source: https://www.chainalysis.com/blog/x402-agentic-payments-adoption/

## Zora (creator/content coins) — content monetization (cautionary example) (build size: 4/5)
- **What:** Protocol turning every post into a tradeable coin paired against the creator's coin; creators earn a share of trading fees on their name and content; powered Base App's mintable feed in 2025.
- **Traction:** Peak Aug 2025: 1.6M creator coins minted, ~3M unique traders, $470M+ cumulative volume, 1.8M+ tokens across ~200K creators, daily creator rewards spiking to ~$375K (Odaily; 0x case study). Then collapse: Feb 2026 Coinbase discontinued Creator Rewards and removed the feed; ZORA market cap fell ~95% ($550M→~$30M); 30-day protocol fees only $13.8K / revenue $5.7K by Aug 31 2026 (DefiLlama via MemeCentral).
- **Revenue:** 1% total trading fee on creator/content coins (0.5% creator, 0.2% market contribution, referral shares; 20% of fees locked as LP), 0.01% on trend coins, 99%→1% sniper tax decaying over 10s (chainwatchdaily; Zora docs).
- **Retention loop:** Failure case: speculative fee-sharing kept traders only while rewards/emotion lasted — attention-token speculation is not a retention loop; durable Base winners move dollars (payments, loyalty, prediction fees).
- Source: https://www.odaily.news/en/post/5211890

## Transferable concepts for a personhood-gated L2

### Auto-tipping rail where one verified human's appreciation is a micro-payment: likes/follows/comments silently send $0.01–0.10 USDC to the au
- Prior art: Noice ($2.14M GMV, 12.75M transactions, ~90% return rate, self-reported at noice.so + Dune Digest 012), Tipn (was #1 trending Farcaster mini app per Bankless), Degen (10% tip take → weekly lottery, Bankless).
- Why proof-of-personhood helps: Base's tipping seasons got farmed by multi-accounting and bot clusters minting engagement for airdrops; with World ID's one-human-one-account, tip budgets, lottery entries and 'most-tipped' leaderboards are guaranteed to map to distinct people, so a small treasury can reward real humans without sybil dilution — and creators know every tip is a person, not a script.
- Fee angle: Platform take on tip flow (Degen precedent: 10%) or a rake on weekly lottery pools; free gas allowance for verified users makes even $0.02 tips economically viable, so rake can be a larger share than on Base where gas eats micro-tips.

### Cross-merchant stamp-card loyalty where the membership NFT is bound to a proof of personhood: eat/shop at any participating local merchant, 
- Prior art: Blackbird on Base ($85M raised, ~1,000 restaurants, 0→10K daily transactions in 2024, 242M+ $FLY distributed; 2% payment fee vs 3.75% card; $89/mo per restaurant).
- Why proof-of-personhood helps: Loyalty fraud (duplicate signups, churning welcome bonuses, bot-claimed promos) is the top cost merchants absorb in every rewards program; one-human-one-membership makes point liability predictable, lets merchants trust shared-cohort data, and turns 'last-minute table' perks into legitimate queue fairness among verified locals instead of power users with 40 accounts.
- Fee angle: 1-2% discount off card interchange charged on in-app payment/redemption + small merchant SaaS fee + breakage float on unredeemed points; the verified-human ledger is the pitch that lets the network charge less fraud contingency than Web2 programs.

### Sybil-resistant group money: rotating savings circles (ROSCA/Ajo-style) and shared-bill escrow where every seat in the circle is a verified 
- Prior art: HyperSplit (receipt-split + instant USDC settle on Base), Splitwise-class trackers (SplitMyExpenses), TheRing/Ajo rotating-savings builds (hackathon-grade, Arc), Splits.org ($250M+ distributed via recurring payment contracts, already deployed on World Chain).
- Why proof-of-personhood helps: A savings circle's only collateral is trust that each member is one real person who cannot dilute obligations across wallets; World ID gives the ring that guarantee and creates a portable, human-verified payment history for unbanked members (25M+ verified humans are exactly the market informal ROSCAs already serve). Bill-splitting also becomes cheat-proof: no fake participants to absorb IOUs.
- Fee angle: Flat micro-fee per round payout (~0.5-1%, pennies on-chain) or keep-the-late-fee model (5% penalty shared by punctual members per TheRing, platform skims a fraction); escrow fees repeat automatically every round, which is the recurring-usage engine.

### Human-only micro-prediction pools: hourly/weekly yes-no markets or survivor pools on crypto, culture and local events where entry is hard-ca
- Prior art: Limitless on Base (volume $5.7M/mo Aug 2025 → $1.66B/mo Apr 2026; 71K users and $1.51M April fees per Crawlux; maker-free CLOB + oracle resolution), Bracky agent-style betting on Farcaster (500%+ net-new users per game via feed-native prompts, Neynar case study).
- Why proof-of-personhood helps: One-human-one-position removes sybil wash-trading and multi-account points-season farming that Onchain Atlas flags as Limitless's core volume risk, gives fair odds to casual users (whales can't corner an hourly market with 50 wallets), doubles as age/eligibility gating without KYC, and makes leaderboard prizes defensible as 'one person, one win'.
- Fee angle: Taker fee per resolution (Limitless proves ~$1.5M/month is collectible at 71K users) or fixed 3-5% rake on pari-mutuel pools; $1 micro-stakes become practical under free gas for verified users, multiplying fee events per user-hour.

## Method notes
Platform context (all cited): Base carried ~32M monthly active users and $4B bridged assets (CryptoSlate, 2025); $5.35B free-floating stablecoins, 81% USDC, and $3.99B organic volume across 12.8M transfers/30d averaging $294 — proof that consumer-scale dollars actually move here (Allium, Nov 2026). Mini-app distribution is the Base App (hundreds of mini apps day one, base.org; PancakeSwap embedded via mini app, blockeden) — structurally identical to World Money's Mini Apps. CAVEATS: (1) Farcaster's own traction numbers conflict — defi-intel.com claims 1M+ DAU Q1 2026 but ankhlabs (citing Romero + TechFlow) says ~250K MAU / <60K DAU by late 2026; treat feed-native mini-app traction as modest and Coinbase's Base App as the real funnel. (2) Zora/creator coins and Base's Creator Rewards were discontinued Feb 2026 — the loud 2025 consumer thesis (attention-as-speculation) failed (30-day protocol revenue ~$5.7K, Aug 2026); winners that survived are utility rails that move USDC: tipping with tiny budgets, loyalty payments, prediction fees, checkout. (3) Games/gambling (Based Slots, UpOnly, lottery apps) dominate the visible mini-app catalog, so the non-game category is genuinely underserved — savings/round-up had NO Base-native winner (closest: Acorns-style Web2 apps; Mamoru and TheRing are hackathon-scale, TVL in hundreds of dollars, UNVERIFIED), as did subscriptions (only the free Base Subscriptions primitive and invoice tools like PayRequest at 2%). Those are open lanes, not proven winners. (4) build_size values are my engineering estimates, not sourced facts. (5) Fee take-rates for Noice/Tipn/HyperSplit are unlisted by the vendors — marked UNVERIFIED inline. Adjacent idea worth stealing: personhood-gated x402 — sell metered access to any API/AI service where each verified human gets a free monthly quota and pays pennies beyond it (x402 already does ~138M micro-settlements/quarter on Base at ~$0.001 facilitator fee; one-human-one-account makes free quotas sybil-proof).
