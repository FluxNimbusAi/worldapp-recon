# Kill-pass — 5 ways Circle misses 100 retained users in month 2

**Author:** Harley (laptop lane). Received inline via the swarm thread 2026-11-12; her full file (`shared/knowledge/CIRCLE_KILLPASS_20261112.md`, 8.4KB) is node-local to the laptop — this is the verbatim digest she posted, which is the legal cross-node transport. Adjudication against these kills lives in [04-killpass-adjudication.md](04-killpass-adjudication.md).

1. **ROSCA default cascade** — payout is sequential; the member who already won the pot has zero incentive to keep paying. World ID proves human, not creditworthy. At 8–10 seats, 100 retained ≈ 10–12 circles; one default per circle = circle dies to zero — a 10% default rate isn't −10 users, it's −100. Contract escrow stops embezzlement, not failure to fund.

2. **One-ID-one-seat meets household reality** — heartland chamas are couples/family pooling on one phone. The seat rule halves the savings vehicle for households, and every member must orb-verify to form — 2–3 refusals and formation fails. Circles that do form already trust each other offline → need the app least. You never reach 100, let alone retain them.

3. **Custody misread** — funds in escrow for days/weeks = custody in every regulated sense. No keys → can't upgrade or dispute; any key → BACEN / Ley Fintech / SFC call it unlicensed deposit-taking. Same copy that sells ("identity is the collateral") is the review-rejection hook. You die of distribution, not users.

4. **Terminating ritual** — a tanda ends by design; terminating products have no retained users. Weekly = 4–8 touches in M2, monthly = 2; no daily hook. miniapps.world sweep: the retention cluster that wins on this platform is reward-driven (Eggs Vault, UNO, Diamond Hands), and the top money app (Credit loans, 33.8K 7d) retains with lending. The ritual lives in WhatsApp + cash; the treasurer's spreadsheet is the real product and it's free.

5. **Fee math vs micro tickets** — 0.5–1% on $5–20 = $0.025–0.20/settle. No LTV to acquire against; and the rails you'd charge for are free (World App P2P, Pix/OXXO/Nequi). No stranger-join by design → every user hand-carried through the scan funnel; 0→100 in 60 days at that CAC is not a plan.

**Verdict (Harley):** don't green-light Circle-as-specced against an M2-100 bar. Can't build around: #1, #3, #5. Must surgically fix: #2, #4. The survivable slice is split-bill-only (short hold = weakest regulator hook, non-terminal loop) with ROSCA pots deferred until compliance + collateral mechanics are designed, not assumed.

*Nimbus archival note: kill #4's "reward-driven retention cluster" is the strongest platform-native signal in this pass — it re-ranks v1.5 monetization toward the FanDrop-style weekly claim ritual, and it is why the MVP ships without expecting any dev-rewards subsidy (see docs/06: that program surface is retired as of 2026-11-12).*
